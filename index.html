Who We Are

Design-led. Family-run. Genuinely ours.

We started Thomson’s Edge because we believe a renovation should begin with design, not demolition. Every project is shaped by a real family who cares how the details come together — from the first mood board to the final cabinet pull.

Because we offer both design and renovation under one roof, you can work with us for a full transformation, a considered design package, or beautiful cabinetry on its own. It’s your project, on your terms.
