Our Story

About Thomson’s Edge

A family-owned studio where design comes first and every client works directly with the people who care most about the result.

Design first, always.

Thomson’s Edge was built on a simple conviction: a beautiful renovation starts with thoughtful design. We’re a family-owned studio, which means the people who plan your space are the same people who stand behind it, start to finish.

That closeness shapes everything. We take on projects we genuinely care about, we sweat the details others overlook, and we treat your home the way we’d treat our own.

Because we offer both design and renovation, we can meet you wherever you are: a full transformation, a design package to build your way, or simply the cabinetry that anchors the room.
