What We Offer

Services

We lead with design and deliver with craft. Work with us for a complete renovation, a design package to build elsewhere, or cabinetry on its own.

Service One

Design

Great rooms are designed before they’re built. Our design service covers space planning, layouts, cabinetry design, finishes, fixtures, and the complete material palette — assembled into a clear, buildable package.

Take that package and build it with our renovation team, or bring it to trades of your own. Either way, you leave with a fully resolved vision, not a pile of guesswork.

Service Two

Renovation

When you’re ready to build, our renovation service turns the design into reality. We manage the kitchen or bathroom project end to end, keeping the same design intent front and centre from demolition through the final reveal.

One team, one point of contact, one cohesive result — without the disconnect that happens when design and construction don’t speak the same language.

Also Available

Cabinetry & design packages, sold individually.

Not every project needs the full journey. We’re one of the few studios happy to provide custom cabinetry on its own, or a standalone design package — giving you exactly the help you need and nothing you don’t.

From kitchens and vanities to laundry rooms, mudrooms and built-ins, our cabinetry is made to fit your space and your life — beautifully finished, and built to last.

How It Works

A calm, considered process.

i.

Consultation

We meet, understand your space, your timeline, and your investment level, and talk through what’s possible.

ii.

Design

We develop layouts, cabinetry, finishes and the full material story into a package you can see and feel.

iii.

Build

Our renovation team brings the design to life — or hands you a package ready to build elsewhere.

iv.

Reveal

We walk through the finished space together and make sure every detail lives up to the vision.
